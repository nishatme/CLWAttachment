CEX CLW is responsible for supporting the materials needed for GOH

the materials are taken stored of, procured by and supplied from this Stores Diesel Depot situated inside CLW.

- All the diesel spares are stored in this depot.
- But for all the non-diesel spares needed for this workshop, parts are sourced from Saidpur General Depot by making requisition from here. In that case, the non-diesel parts are not stocked in the depot, rather is supplied directly to the workshop.

- 15 classes of parts (more than 30,000 items) of diesel spare parts (locomotive spare parts).
- all are classified in the BRASS-I (Bangladesh Railway Automated Support System I) software
- Kit List basis indent for CLW
- PI (primary indent)
- after vetting -> DIC ICC -> update in BRASS I

either
- DIC (ICC) -> MTI (Material Transfer Instructor)

- Purchase section (P-2) responsible for purchase of diesel spares. (this is a wing of the CCS PHT)
